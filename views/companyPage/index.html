About Tech Solutions Inc.

Tech Solutions Inc. is a leading technology company specializing in cloud computing, software development, and IT services. We are committed to innovation and security, and we value the contributions of the security community in helping us identify and address potential vulnerabilities. Our bug bounty program is designed to reward researchers for their efforts in improving the security of our products and services.

Bug Bounty Program Details

Scope

Our bug bounty program covers a wide range of our products and services, including our cloud platform, web applications, and mobile apps. For a detailed list of in-scope and out-of-scope assets, please refer to the 'Rules' tab.

Rewards

We offer competitive rewards based on the severity of the reported vulnerability. Rewards range from $500 to $10,000, with higher rewards for critical vulnerabilities. See the 'Rules' tab for a detailed breakdown of reward amounts.

Severity

Vulnerabilities are classified into four severity levels: Critical, High, Medium, and Low. The severity level is determined based on the potential impact of the vulnerability and the likelihood of exploitation. See the 'Rules' tab for more information on severity classifications.

Announcements

Program Update: New Reward Structure

We've updated our reward structure to better reflect the value of high-impact vulnerabilities. Critical vulnerabilities now offer rewards up to $10,000.
